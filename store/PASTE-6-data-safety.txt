PLAY CONSOLE ▸ App content ▸ Data safety
Update required before shipping v218 (Google Sheet sync).
=========================================================

WHY THIS CHANGES
Until v217 the declaration said Daylog collects no data, and that was true — nothing left
the device. v218 adds an OPT-IN Google Sheet sync. When a user connects it, their log is
transmitted off the device to a Google Apps Script they deployed in their own Google
account. That is "collected" under Play's definition (transmitted off the device), even
though it goes to the user's own Drive and the developer never receives it.

Leaving the old declaration in place while shipping the feature is a policy violation.
Update the form BEFORE the release that turns sync on.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — Data collection and security
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
  → YES

Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
  → YES
    (Apps Script web-app endpoints are HTTPS only.)

Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?
  → YES
    (The sheet is in the user's own Drive; deleting it deletes the copy. Clearing the link
     in Settings stops all transmission. Both are stated in the app.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — Data types
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For EVERY type below, the answers are the same:
  Collected: YES        Shared: NO
  Processed ephemerally: NO
  Required or optional: OPTIONAL — users can choose whether this data is collected
  Purpose: App functionality  (do NOT tick Analytics, Advertising, or Personalization)

Tick these types:

  Personal info ▸ Name
    Only if the user types a name in Settings. It is optional and free-text.

  Health and fitness ▸ Health info
    Journal/log fields the USER TYPES: mood, energy, sleep hours they enter by hand,
    workout log, habit ticks.
    ⚠ Health CONNECT readings (steps, distance, heart rate, sleep sessions, calories,
      workouts) are NOT transmitted — dp.health is deliberately excluded from the sync
      payload. Do not declare those as collected, and do not start sending them without
      revisiting this form and the Health Connect terms.

  Personal info ▸ Other personal info
    Free-text journal and reflection entries, notes, plans, project notes.

  App activity ▸ Other actions
    Time-tracking blocks, tasks, goals, awards.

Do NOT tick: Location, Financial info, Messages, Photos/Videos, Audio, Files,
Contacts, Calendar, Web browsing, App info and performance, Device or other IDs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — Things to check yourself, because I could not verify them
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Play may treat a destination the USER configures and controls differently from a
   developer-run server. If such an exemption exists it could simplify the above. I could
   not confirm the current policy text, so everything here is the CONSERVATIVE answer:
   declaring more than strictly required is safe; declaring less is not.

2. Health Connect has its own terms on top of Data Safety, and they are stricter about
   moving health data off the device. This is exactly why dp.health is excluded from the
   sync. If you ever want Health Connect data in the sheet, read those terms first — do
   not just add the key to the payload.

3. The privacy policy URL in the listing must describe the sync too. Updated copy is in
   privacy.html; re-upload it wherever the listing points.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 — Order of operations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Publish the updated privacy.html.
2. Update Data safety as above and SAVE (it can be submitted without a new release).
3. Only then roll out the build with sync visible.
Doing 3 before 2 is the thing that gets apps pulled.
