A habit tracker, goal tracker, mood log and screen time tracker in one — completely offline, with no account and no cloud.

Daylog is a private habit tracker and daily log for people who want to understand their days. Track habits and streaks, set any goal, log your mood and energy, see your screen time, time your focus — and get real insights back. No sign-up, no data collected, nothing uploaded.

Insights that actually know you
Most trackers draw charts. Daylog reads your data on your phone and tells you what works for you:
• "Your sleep sweet spot is 7–8h — mood 7.8 vs 5.9 after short nights"
• "Active days buy you 26 more minutes of sleep"
• "Walking today → mood +0.7 tomorrow" — next-day effects, with a confidence rating
Every insight uses honest minimum sample sizes, measures you against your own averages rather than someone else's, and never leaves your phone.

Habit tracker with streaks — and a skip button
A daily checklist with streak counts, 90-day heatmaps and consistency stats. Create, rename, colour and reorder every habit — your routine, not a template.

Rest days shouldn't punish you. Tap a habit twice to skip it: your streak stays alive and the day leaves your completion rate. There's a weekly streak too — log any 3 days and the week counts — so one bad Tuesday costs nothing. Every habit gets a strength score out of 100 — a weighted trend, so one missed day can't wipe out months and one good day can't fake it. And you can give a habit a when-and-where cue — "after my morning coffee, at my desk" — which shows until you tick it off. Naming the moment is the best-evidenced habit trick there is.

Goals — all of them, free
Any target across nine measures, over a week, a month or a year. Each goal shows what's left and how much per day gets there, and can suggest one from what you actually averaged before. Other trackers charge for this.

Your trophy case
Sixty achievements across nine families, tiered so a big goal pays out along the way. Worked out from your own log — nothing fakeable, nothing ever taken away.

You vs you
Compare your last week, last four weeks, or the same four weeks a year ago against your own past. No leaderboards, no percentiles, nobody else's numbers — the only person on the other side is you.

Screen time, tracked for you
Your phone usage is measured automatically and charted against your mood. Every auto-tracked signal has an on/off switch.

A one-tap daily time tracker
Tap an activity when you start — work, travel, gym, scrolling — and tap the next when you switch. Your day becomes a colour-coded 24-hour timeline, and tracked sleep and work fill in your log. Stop a timer and note what it was and how it felt.

Month in review
A five-card wrap-up of any month you've logged: habits ranked, how it felt, where the time went. Nothing expires. And your entry from a week, a month or a year ago resurfaces at the top of today's log.

Mood journal and private journal
Log mood and energy, answer short reflections, write a full entry — typing or voice. Add notes, plans and articles. Search everything you've written, #tags included.

Focus timer and gym log
Pomodoro with an ongoing notification, timeboxing and focus sounds. Plus a workout log: editable split, animated demos, per-set logging and muscle-group breakdowns. Daily reminders arrive as notifications or full-screen alarms, plus calendar events.

Truly private, truly offline
• No account, no cloud, no sync, no data collected
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics
• Works 100% offline
• Export any time (JSON, CSV or a PDF report)

Make it yours
Light, dark and black themes plus Auto. Pin whichever tabs you want. Rename, hide or reorder any field, section, habit or activity — including every motivational part, if you'd rather not.
