PLAY CONSOLE ▸ App content ▸ Health apps ▸ STEP 2: Health data permissions
Daylog requests SIX Health Connect permissions, all READ-ONLY.
Paste the matching block into each permission's justification box.
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

── ACTIVITY (3 permissions) ────────────────────────────────────────

▸ READ_STEPS
Daylog is a private daily-log app. It reads your step count so the user does not
have to type it in, shows it on their daily log, and correlates it with the mood
and energy they record themselves — for example "the day after your more active
days your mood averages +0.7". It also powers a personal "most steps" record.
All processing happens on the device. The data is never transmitted, uploaded or
shared, there is no account and no server, and step reading has its own on/off
switch in Settings.

▸ READ_DISTANCE
Shown alongside steps on the user's daily log so their day is recorded without
manual entry. Read-only, processed entirely on the device, never transmitted, and
individually switchable off in Settings.

▸ READ_EXERCISE
Read to fill in active minutes on the user's daily log automatically and to
correlate exercise with the energy levels they log themselves. It also powers a
personal "most active minutes" record. Read-only, on-device only, never
transmitted, and individually switchable off in Settings.

── NUTRITION (1 permission) ────────────────────────────────────────

▸ READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED
Read to show calories burned on the user's daily log without manual entry, and to
correlate it with the energy they record. Daylog does not track food, diet or
weight. Read-only, on-device only, never transmitted, and individually switchable
off in Settings.

── SLEEP (1 permission) ────────────────────────────────────────────

▸ READ_SLEEP
Sleep is central to the app. Daylog reads sleep duration so the user's sleep is
logged without manual entry, and uses it to find their personal patterns — their
"sleep sweet spot" (which sleep duration precedes their best mood), how much extra
sleep follows an active day, and what a heavy screen-time day costs the following
night. It also powers a personal "longest sleep" record. All computed on the
device. Read-only, never transmitted, and switchable off in Settings.

── VITALS (1 permission) ───────────────────────────────────────────

▸ READ_HEART_RATE
Read to show the user's average heart rate on their daily log and to surface one
recovery insight — how much higher their heart rate runs after a short night's
sleep. Daylog makes no medical or diagnostic claims and does not detect any
condition. Read-only, on-device only, never transmitted, and individually
switchable off in Settings.
