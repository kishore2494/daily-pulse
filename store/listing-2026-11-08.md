## Full description (2,379 / 4,000)
```text
Daylog is a private lifelog for your days: a habit tracker, mood journal and memory book in one app. It works offline, with no account, and now comes in five looks you choose yourself.

Memories, the way you would keep them in a notebook
Give every moment its own page: the story, the date, the place, who was there, tags, and all its photos and videos. Link pages to each other, browse them year by year, and see what happened on this day in earlier years. Import a batch of photos and Daylog sorts them into one memory per day they were taken.

Five looks. Pick yours.
Midnight, Paper, Luxe, Aurora or Bloom. Not a colour swap: each style has its own typeface, cards and colours. Paper is as clean as a notebook page, Luxe is black and champagne gold, Aurora puts northern lights behind frosted glass, Bloom is soft and warm. Change it any time.

Habits you can actually keep
Tick a habit in one tap, from the Log or the Habits screen. Every week becomes a column in your heatmap. Skip a rest day and the streak stays alive; a strength score shows the trend so one missed day never erases months.

How do you actually feel?
One square on the mood grid sets your mood and your energy, then Daylog hands you the words for it. Write a journal entry, add #tags, photos or a voice note.

It tells you what really works
Daylog finds your own patterns, worked out on your phone: your sleep sweet spot, which habit lifts your mood the next day, what heavy screen days cost you. Every insight waits for enough days to be honest.

Your whole day, one tap at a time
Tap Work, then Gym, then Sleep. Your day draws itself on a 24-hour timeline, and the hours you track fill your log.

Find anything you ever wrote
Search every journal, note, memory and photo caption in a second, even offline. History and a calendar let you reread any day.

And the rest
A focus timer, workout log, money tracker, tasks, plans, projects, notes and reminders. Undo for anything you delete by mistake.

Private by design
• No account, no sign-up
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics
• Your log, photos and voice notes stay on your phone
• One backup file holds your log, photos and voice notes (videos stay on the phone); export CSV or a PDF report
• Nothing is locked. Optional tips help keep Daylog going.

Daylog keeps no cloud copy, so uninstalling erases your data. Keep your backup file somewhere safe.
```
